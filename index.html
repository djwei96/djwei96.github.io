Dongjun Wei

I hold a PhD from the Faculty of Business and Economics at HKU and previously earned my master's degree at the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I also served as a visiting scholar at the Alfred Lerner College of Business & Economics, University of Delaware.

My research focuses on generative AI, LLMs, trustworthy AI, healthcare, and data mining, bridging information systems and computer science to tackle real-world problems in business and society. I am currently an Assistant Professor in the Information and Operations Management (IOM) Department at ESCP Business School (Paris).
